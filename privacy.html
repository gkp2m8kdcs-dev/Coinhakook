Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Short version: Coinhakook does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Your portfolio, transactions, and notes are stored locally on your device. The app requires no account and uses no tracking SDKs.

1. Introduction

Coinhakook (“the App”, “we”, “our”) is a privacy-first personal crypto portfolio journal developed and distributed for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles — and, more importantly, what it does not.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number).
  • No account credentials — the App has no sign-up or login.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or behavioral tracking.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA) or third-party SDKs that profile you.
  • No location data.
  • No device fingerprinting.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data only on your device, inside the app’s encrypted sandbox:

  • Transactions you log (buys, sells, staking rewards, airdrops, transfers), including venue, fee, tag, and note fields you choose to enter.
  • Holdings derived from your transactions.
  • Watchlist items and app preferences (theme, base currency).

This data is never sent to us or to any third party. It remains on your device unless you actively export or back it up using iOS features you control (e.g., iCloud Backup of device data, which is governed by Apple’s privacy terms).

4. Market & Price Data

Coinhakook may fetch non-personal public market data (such as prices or coin metadata) from third-party crypto data providers to power charts and analytics. These requests contain no personal identifiers and are limited to publicly available market information.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Your Rights and Control

Because we do not collect any data from you, there is nothing to access, delete, or export from our side. All data lives on your device, and you may erase it at any time by deleting the App or using the in-app reset options.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside the App. Material changes will never silently expand the scope of data collection.
