Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Coinhakook works completely offline and requires no sign-up. All data lives on your device.

Where is my data stored?

Your transactions, watchlists, and settings are stored locally in the app’s encrypted sandbox on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Use the export option in Settings to save a portable copy of your journal. You can re-import it on the same or another device.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your device to remove all locally stored data. You can also use the in-app reset option in Settings.

Does Coinhakook connect to my exchange?

No. The app does not connect to exchanges, wallets, or APIs that require credentials. All entries are manual.

Which devices are supported?

Coinhakook is designed for iPhone on iOS 16 and above. A dark-mode interface is the default.

I found a bug — how do I report it?

Please email ecekibi725@gmail.com with a description, your iOS version, and your device model.

Is Coinhakook free?

Pricing and availability are shown on the App Store listing. The app may offer optional in-app purchases.

Troubleshooting Tips

  • Make sure your iPhone is running iOS 16 or later.
  • If charts don’t load, pull-to-refresh on the Markets tab.
  • If you accidentally reset data, restore from your most recent export file.
  • For accurate PnL, double-check that fees and notes are entered on every transaction.
